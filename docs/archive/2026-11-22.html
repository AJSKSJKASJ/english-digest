📰 句子 1 The Guardian · World

The British-led Cobre Mining Company helped industrialise Manchester via copper deposits and enslaved African people • Slave-trade wealth was embedded in Britain’s financial system, research finds

🧠 拆解主语是Company，核心动作是helped industrialise和enslaved，难点是via引导的方式状语和破折号后的独立句。

💡 精讲

  • 短语enslave = 奴役（动词），不是“奴隶”名词
  • 防坑别把enslaved当形容词“被奴役的”，这里作谓语动词。
  • 造句They enslaved workers for decades.
📰 句子 2 The Guardian · World

So the University of Glasgow social scientist was horrified to learn that her reputation and even her photograph had been hijacked by rogue casinos claiming her endorsement for the very games whose addictive properties she has documented.

🧠 拆解主语是scientist，核心是was horrified to learn that...，难点是that从句套claiming分词和whose定语从句。

💡 精讲

  • 短语hijack = 盗用/劫持（熟词生义，非“劫机”）
  • 防坑别译成“被劫持”，这里指名誉和照片被冒用。
  • 造句Her name was hijacked by scammers.
📰 句子 3 The Guardian · Sport

It is not a laugh-your-head-off laugh, more a chuckle, a giggle, as if amused at everything he did. And for all the self-deprecation, the lack of triumphalism, he did everything.

🧠 拆解主语it指laugh，核心是is not...more...，难点是as if amused省略结构和for all让步短语。

💡 精讲

  • 短语for all = 尽管（让步），不是“为了所有”
  • 防坑别把for all译成“为了全部”，这里表让步。
  • 造句For all his faults, he is kind.
