📰 句子 1 The Guardian
结构 · 非限制性定语从句 · 分词后置

前情文章讲一名无资质女子做美容手术致人毁容。这句描述受害者：她做过眼睑手术，而手术本身有风险。

She was a woman in her 40s who had undergone a blepharoplasty, a procedure that involves making an incision just below or above the eyelids and removing small amounts of skin and sometimes fat and muscle.

🧠 拆解主干 She was a woman；who 从句修饰 woman；逗号后 a procedure 是同位语，解释 blepharoplasty，其中 that 从句再解释 procedure。

💡 精讲

  • 短语a procedure that involves doing sth（一项涉及做某事的程序）
  • 防坑别把 a procedure 误当成修饰 eyelids 的定语，它其实是同位语，回指 blepharoplasty。
  • 造句It was a surgery that involves removing small amounts of tissue.
📰 句子 2 The New Yorker
文采 · 比喻 · 长句节奏

前情文章回顾独立电影史，这句评价一部被忽视的独立片如何影响后来者。

It isn’t only Bronstein’s personal involvement that has contributed to the Safdies’ successes; it’s also the explosive power of “Frownland” itself, which, despite a token release, blew a hole in the cinematic ozone layer through which the Safdies leaped headlong.

🧠 拆解用“在电影臭氧层炸开一个洞”比喻一部小片撕开行业缺口，后接 through which 从句写后来者纵身跃入，画面与节奏俱佳。

💡 精讲

  • 短语blow a hole in sth（在……上炸开缺口，喻打破僵局）
  • 防坑别直译成“炸了臭氧层”，要保留比喻义：撕开缺口、打破壁垒。
  • 造句Her debut blew a hole in the indie scene.
📰 句子 3 The Atlantic
结构 · 让步从句 · 破折号插入 · 名词性从句

前情文章批评特朗普团队对俄绥靖。这句点出核心：库什纳认为战争无关对错，作者反驳说侵略者与受害者界限分明。

One key to Kushner’s view is a throwaway line: “This isn’t about fairness or who’s right or wrong.” To the contrary, this is about a war in which the distinction between aggressor and victim, predator and prey could not be clearer.

🧠 拆解先引一句轻描淡写的话，再用 To the contrary 反转；in which 从句里 between...and... 并列，could not be clearer 用否定比较表最高级。

💡 精讲

  • 短语could not be clearer（再清楚不过了）
  • 防坑别把 could not be clearer 理解成“不能更清楚”的字面否定，它是强调“极其清楚”。
  • 造句The line between right and wrong could not be clearer.
