📰 句子 1 The Guardian
让步从句 · 非限制性定语从句 · 分词后置

前情讲 Power Slap 比赛现场：选手被分楼层隔离，防止私下斗殴。这句在描述赛前安排，值得读是因为从句套从句，主谓宾被隔开。

At the Crowne Plaza hotel, where competitors were booked to stay, those facing off had been separated by floor, lest any unauthorised violence took place.

🧠 拆解主干是 those facing off had been separated by floor；where 从句插在 hotel 后，lest 从句表目的，facing off 是后置定语。

💡 精讲

  • 短语lest any ... took place（以防发生……）
  • 防坑别把 where 从句误当成修饰 floor；lest 后接虚拟语气，别写成 will take place。
  • 造句They left early, lest any trouble arose.
📰 句子 2 The New Yorker
比喻+画面

前情作者写挪威主权基金掌门人 Tangen 的做派：他要把这个庞大基金说成是缓慢而稳健的巨轮，由一位温和的船长掌舵。

He clearly wanted to give the impression that the oljefond was like one of those massive cruise ships which ply the country's fjords—big and slow, with a benign captain guiding it.

🧠 拆解主干：He wanted to give the impression that + 从句。从句里用 like 引出明喻：基金 = 航行在峡湾里的巨型邮轮。破折号后 big and slow, with a benign captain guiding it 三个短促成分收尾，节奏由长句骤然收紧，画面定格在“大、慢、有温和船长”。

💡 精讲

  • 短语ply the country's fjords = （船只）定期往返于该国峡湾；benign captain = 温和的船长（隐喻掌舵人）
  • 防坑ply 在这里不是“供应/努力”，而是“（船）定期航行于”；benign 不是“良性（肿瘤）”，而是“温和、不具威胁的”。
  • 造句He wanted the project to feel like a lighthouse on a rocky coast—steady, unglamorous, quietly keeping others off the rocks.
📰 句子 3 The Atlantic
让步从句 · 破折号插入语 · 名词性从句

前情文章质疑 Nvidia 黄仁勋复职的安全审查：一个高管与外国情报有染，却一周内被放行。这句用反讽收束全段。

An executive working with some of America’s most exotic technology commits adultery with a foreign citizen who has ties to a hostile government, and is credibly accused of funneling highly restricted American technology to a hostile intelligence service—and the U.S. security mandarins give the guy a thumbs up after a week?

🧠 拆解两个并列谓语堆出严重指控，破折号后突然甩出轻佻的 thumbs up，用反差制造反讽。

💡 精讲

  • 短语give sb a thumbs up（给某人放行/点赞）
  • 防坑别把 and 连接的第二个谓语误读成新句子；破折号后是同一句的反讽落点。
  • 造句He broke every rule, and they gave him a thumbs up.
